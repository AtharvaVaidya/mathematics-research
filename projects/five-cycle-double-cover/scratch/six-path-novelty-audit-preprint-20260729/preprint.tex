\begin{abstract}
We isolate a finite positive boundary relation for two ordered six-poles
obtained by deleting two vertices from the two Blanu\v{s}a snarks.  Edges are
labelled by the ten weight-two vectors of $\F_2^5$, the standard flow encoding
of five labelled even subgraphs covering every edge twice.  Six particular
three-edge boundary states have the property that every ordered concatenation
of two such states extends through either six-pole, for every port order.
This gives a constructive composition lemma.  A solver-free proof table lists
all $2\cdot36^2=2592$ internal extensions, and a separately implemented checker
verifies the graph encodings and every parity equation.  As one application,
a canonical census verifies the hypothesis for 9,278 independent
junction-set orbits in cyclically 4-edge-connected simple cubic macros of
order at most 14.  This is a restricted local and finite result; it neither
proves nor disproves the Five-Cycle Double Cover Conjecture.
\end{abstract}

\section{Five labelled even subgraphs as a duad flow}

All graphs in the theorem below are finite.  For a graph $G$, an
\emph{even subgraph} means an edge subset having even degree at every vertex;
it need not be connected and may be empty.  This is the convention under
which a labelled five-cycle double cover is a tuple
$(C_0,\ldots,C_4)$ of even subgraphs such that every edge belongs to exactly
two members of the tuple.

Let
\[
 D=\{x\in\F_2^5:\wt(x)=2\}.
\]
We identify a vector supported on $\{i,j\}$ with the duad $ij$.

\begin{proposition}[standard duad formulation]\label{prop:duad}
A graph $G$ has five labelled even subgraphs covering every edge exactly twice
if and only if there is a map $\lambda:E(G)\to D$ such that
\[
 \bigoplus_{e\ni v}\lambda(e)=0
 \qquad\text{for every }v\in V(G),
\]
where a loop is counted twice in the incidence sum.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Given $(C_0,\ldots,C_4)$, let the $i$th coordinate of $\lambda(e)$ indicate
whether $e\in C_i$.  Exact double coverage gives $\wt(\lambda(e))=2$, and
even degree in every $C_i$ gives the displayed xor equation coordinate by
coordinate.  Conversely, take $C_i$ to be the edges whose labels have
$i$th coordinate one.  The xor equations make every $C_i$ even and the
weight-two condition gives exact double coverage.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:duad} is background, not a novelty claim.  Equivalent
weight-two flow formulations occur in the FiveCDC literature; see, in
particular, Hu\v{s}ek and \v{S}\'amal~\cite{husek-samal-2026}.

\section{Ordered six-poles and their boundary relations}

An ordered six-pole $X$ consists here of a cubic graph fragment with six
ordered semiedges.  Its internal vertices have degree three after semiedges
are counted.  A word $w=(w_1,\ldots,w_6)\in D^6$ is \emph{extendable through
$X$} if the internal edges can be labelled from $D$ so that the xor of
incident labels is zero at every internal vertex.  Let
\[
 R_X=\{w\in D^6:w\text{ is extendable through }X\}.
\]

The two fragments are fixed by the following machine-independent data.  The
graph6 strings encode the source graphs; vertices are numbered from zero.
After deletion, the verifier canonically relabels the remaining vertices and
recovers the displayed ordered port vertices.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
atom & source graph6 & deleted source vertices & ordered ports after relabelling\\
\midrule
$A$ & \texttt{Q??CA?\_CCOW\_Q\_M?AD@A\_@K?F??} & $4,9$ &
$(8,10,11,3,13,14)$\\
$B$ & \texttt{Q???C@?GCoOoDO[?CcAO\_?k?J??} & $13,16$ &
$(1,2,3,6,8,9)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each fragment has 16 internal vertices, 21 internal edges, and six
semiedges.

\section{The six path states}

Fix the coordinate $0$ and put $Y=\{1,2,3,4\}$.  If
$\{a,b\}\subset Y$ and $\{c,d\}=Y\setminus\{a,b\}$, define the unordered
three-duad state
\[
 P_{ab}=\{0a,0b,cd\}.
\]
Thus the six states are
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\{01,02,34\},&\{01,03,24\},&\{01,04,23\},\\
\{02,03,14\},&\{02,04,13\},&\{03,04,12\}.
\end{array}
\]
Let $\mathcal P_{\rm ord}\subset D^3$ contain all orderings of these
states.  It has $6\cdot3!=36$ members.

\begin{lemma}[two-atom path universality]\label{lem:local}
For each $X\in\{A,B\}$,
\[
 \mathcal P_{\rm ord}\times\mathcal P_{\rm ord}\subseteq R_X.
\]
In particular, every one of the $36^2=1296$ ordered boundary words extends
through each atom.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Finite proof certificate]
The file \texttt{path-atom-witnesses.json} in the accompanying frozen
artifact contains one row for each of the 1,296 boundary words and each atom.
A row contains the boundary word followed by 21 internal duads in the listed
internal-edge order.  For every row, direct inspection consists only of:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item checking that each boundary and internal label is a five-bit integer
        of Hamming weight two;
  \item placing the six boundary labels on the displayed ordered ports; and
  \item xoring the three incident labels at each of the 16 internal vertices.
\end{enumerate}
All resulting vertex xors are zero.  Hence each row is an explicit extension.
The accompanying solver-free program \texttt{verify\_frontier.py} performs
exactly these checks after reconstructing both source graphs from their
graph6 strings.  It imports no producer module and calls no SAT solver.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is a checkable computer-assisted proof: the table supplies witnesses,
not an assertion that a search returned ``satisfiable.''  It is not yet a
compact hand proof of all 2,592 rows.  A symmetry reduction to a small set of
conceptual cases remains desirable.
\end{remark}

\section{The composition lemma}

Let $M$ be a cubic macro graph whose vertices are partitioned into
\emph{junctions} and \emph{connectors}.  Suppose the connectors are paired.
Replace each pair by either $A$ or $B$, attaching the three incident macro
edges of the first connector to the first port block and those of the second
connector to the second block, in arbitrary orders.  Junctions and macro
edges remain.

\begin{theorem}[six-state composition]\label{thm:composition}
Suppose the macro edges have labels in $D$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the three labels incident with every junction xor to zero; and
  \item the unordered labels incident with every connector form one of the
        six states $P_{ab}$.
\end{enumerate}
Then every connector matching, every assignment of $A$ or $B$ to the pairs,
every ordering of each three-port block, and either orientation of each pair
produces an expanded graph with five labelled even subgraphs covering every
edge exactly twice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Consider one matched connector pair.  Its ordered six boundary labels form
an element of $\mathcal P_{\rm ord}\times\mathcal P_{\rm ord}$.  By
Lemma~\ref{lem:local}, choose the saved internal completion for the selected
atom and port orders.  Make this choice independently for every pair.

The two occurrences of every surviving macro edge retain the same label.
The vertex xor is zero at each junction by hypothesis and at each atom vertex
by the selected local completion.  The result is therefore a $D$-valued
flow on the expanded graph.  Proposition~\ref{prop:duad} converts it to the
required five even subgraphs.
\end{proof}

\section{A finite cyclically 4-edge-connected application}

A connected cubic macro is called cyclically 4-edge-connected if no edge cut
of size at most three has a cycle on both shores.  For every even, nonempty,
independent junction set, connector vertices are paired arbitrarily in the
expansion above.

\begin{theorem}[verified macro frontier]\label{thm:census}
For each cyclically 4-edge-connected simple cubic macro of order
$6,8,10,12$, or $14$, and each automorphism orbit of even nonempty independent
junction sets, there is a macro-edge labelling satisfying the hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:composition}.  Consequently every associated expansion has
a five-cycle double cover, for all connector matchings, atom choices, and
port orders.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Reproducible finite census]
The source graphs are the canonical output of
\texttt{geng -cq -d3 -D3 n}.  Shore enumeration applies the stated cyclic
edge-connectivity definition, full automorphism groups act on the junction
sets, and the artifact stores a positive macro-edge witness for every
representative.  The counts are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr|r}
n&6&8&10&12&14&\text{total}\\ \hline
\text{connected cubic sources}&2&5&19&85&509&620\\
\text{cyclically 4-connected macros}&1&2&5&18&84&110\\
\text{junction-set orbits}&1&5&32&445&8795&9278.
\end{array}
\]
For every saved witness, direct xor checks establish the two hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:composition}.  The independent verifier rebuilds the census,
automorphism orbits, and all witness checks.
\end{proof}

\section{Scope and limitations}

Theorem~\ref{thm:census} is not a reduction of general FiveCDC to macros of
order at most 14.  No induction showing that every cyclically
4-edge-connected cubic macro has a six-state labelling is known.  Indeed, a
diagnostic enlargement to all 3-edge-connected macros through order 14 has
one six-state failure on a macro with a nontrivial cyclic 3-edge cut; adding
one compatible star state repairs the diagnostic census.  That negative
diagnostic is not a FiveCDC counterexample and is not used in any theorem
above.

No target graph was found for which the direct five-coordinate SAT/XOR
formula is unsatisfiable.  Therefore this note provides neither an UNSAT
certificate nor a disproof of FiveCDC.  It also does not address the
orientable FiveCDC conjecture.

\section{Relation to prior work and novelty status}

Multipoles, their semiedge states, and their use in snark reductions are
standard~\cite{nedela-skoviera,fiol-vilaltella}.  Hu\v{s}ek and
\v{S}\'amal~\cite{husek-samal-counting} give the closest general framework:
ordered
multipoles, circuit-double-cover boundaries, multiplicity vectors, and linear
gluing.  Their boundaries remember path identities in circuit double covers
with an unrestricted number of circuits.  Our boundary instead fixes five
named even subgraphs and assigns each semiedge the two names containing it.

Liu, Hao, Luo, and Zhang~\cite{liu-hao-luo-zhang} develop a recent
FiveCDC superposition method using nowhere-zero 4-flows and Catlin
reduction, and apply it to several snark families.  This is close prior art
for the global composition strategy.  We found no ordered weight-two
$\F_2^5$ six-pole state relation or either explicit Blanu\v{s}a atom table
in the inspected material.

Normal and Petersen edge colorings provide another related five-color
language~\cite{mazzuoccolo-mkrtchyan,pirot-sereni-skrekovski}, but their edge
labels and local normality conditions are not the weight-two $\F_2^5$ flow
condition used here.

Our bounded search of primary sources found no publication containing
Lemma~\ref{lem:local} for these two ordered Blanu\v{s}a fragments.  The honest
status is therefore \emph{possible local novelty, not established novelty}.
Old theses, unpublished tables, and differently named multipole computations
remain material gaps in the audit.

\section{AI-use and review disclosure}\label{sec:ai}

The human researcher selected the conjecture, set the research and publication
direction, and requested explicit certification and honest disclosure.
Collaborating OpenAI Codex agents wrote essentially all search programs and
checker code, generated the witness tables, conducted the bounded literature
search, and drafted this manuscript.  The phrase ``independent verifier''
means a separately implemented code path; it does not mean an independently
authored human verification.  As of the date on the title page, no human
expert has line-by-line verified the proof, reproduced the computation from a
clean checkout, or assessed the novelty claim.  These facts should remain in
any public version, updated only to record reviews that actually occurred.
