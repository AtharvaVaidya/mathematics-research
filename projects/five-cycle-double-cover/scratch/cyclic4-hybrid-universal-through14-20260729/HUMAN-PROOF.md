# A six-state composition lemma and the cyclically-4 frontier

## Scope

This note proves a finite theorem about one hybrid construction built from
two explicit Blanuša six-poles.  It does **not** prove the Five-Cycle Double
Cover Conjecture for arbitrary graphs.

All graphs in the macro census are finite, simple, connected, and cubic.
A macro is cyclically 4-edge-connected when no edge cut of size at most
three has a cycle on both shores.  Its junction set `J` is independent,
nonempty, and even.  The other macro vertices are connectors and may be
paired arbitrarily into six-port atoms.

## Duad formulation

Represent a two-subset of `{0,1,2,3,4}` by its five-bit mask.  A macro or
expanded edge receives one duad label.  Bit `i` is the indicator that the
edge belongs to coordinate `i`.

At a completed cubic vertex, xor zero of its three duad labels says that
each coordinate has even degree.  Because every edge label has exactly two
bits, the five coordinates are Eulerian edge-subsets and every edge occurs
in exactly two of them.  Conversely, any such five Eulerian edge-subsets
give one duad per edge and xor zero at every vertex.  This establishes the
exact equivalence used here.

## Six path states

Fix the coordinate `0` and put `Y={1,2,3,4}`.  For every two-subset
`{a,b}` of `Y`, write `{c,d}=Y-{a,b}` and define the unordered connector
state

```text
P_ab = { 0a, 0b, cd }.
```

There are six states:

```text
{01,02,34}  {01,03,24}  {01,04,23}
{02,03,14}  {02,04,13}  {03,04,12}.
```

Every state has xor `Y`.  Thus any two connector states have combined xor
zero.  More importantly, every ordered concatenation of two states extends
through either of the two atoms used here, for every permutation of the
three ports in each block.

This last assertion has a fully explicit positive proof.  There are 36
ordered connector states and therefore 1,296 ordered six-port boundary
words.  `path-atom-witnesses.json` contains a 21-internal-edge completion
for each word and each atom:

```text
atom A: graph6 Q??CA?_CCOW_Q_M?AD@A_@K?F??, delete vertices 4,9
atom B: graph6 Q???C@?GCoOoDO[?CcAO_?k?J??, delete vertices 13,16
```

The independent checker reconstructs both source graphs, performs the
deletions, recovers the six ports, and checks every internal label and
every vertex xor.  No negative relation classification or SAT answer is
needed to verify this positive local lemma.

## Composition lemma

Suppose the macro edges can be labelled so that:

1. the three labels at every junction xor to zero; and
2. the three labels at every connector form one of the six path states.

Then every connector matching, every assignment of atom A or B to the
pairs, every ordering of each connector's three ports, and either
orientation of each pair has a FiveCDC labelling after expansion.

Proof: a matched pair of connectors presents one of the 1,296 ordered
boundary words.  Select its saved completion for the chosen atom.  On
every direct macro edge, the two dangling labels agree because they came
from the same macro-edge label.  At every new junction, xor is zero by
hypothesis.  The saved internal completions give xor zero at every atom
vertex.  Hence the completions glue to a duad labelling of the expanded
graph.  The duad equivalence above gives the five Eulerian edge-subsets.

This is an ordinary finite, constructive proof.  The files make every
local choice explicit.

## Canonical census theorem

The source files were generated by

```text
geng -cq -d3 -D3 n
```

for `n=6,8,10,12,14`.  The connected cubic counts are respectively

```text
2, 5, 19, 85, 509.
```

An independently implemented shore enumeration applies the cyclically-4
definition.  Full automorphism groups are then reconstructed, and
independent positive-even junction sets are quotiented by those groups.

| order | cyclically-4 macros | junction orbits | girth >= 5 macros | girth >= 5 orbits |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 8 | 2 | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| 10 | 5 | 32 | 1 | 2 |
| 12 | 18 | 445 | 2 | 20 |
| 14 | 84 | 8,795 | 9 | 433 |
| **total** | **110** | **9,278** | **12** | **455** |

For every one of the 9,278 junction orbits,
`path-core-report.json` gives a macro-edge labelling satisfying the two
conditions of the composition lemma.  `verify_frontier.py` reconstructs
the entire census without importing producer code and checks every saved
macro and atom witness.

Therefore:

> Every hybrid construction in this cyclically-4 simple cubic macro
> census through order 14 has a FiveCDC labelling, for every connector
> matching, every A/B atom assignment, and every port order.

The girth-at-least-five subcensus is included separately in the table and
has the same conclusion.

## What the cut analysis did and did not prove

Earlier matching-universal failures at orders 10 and 12 were confined to
macros with 2-edge cuts.  The present cyclically-4 census has no such
cuts, and the stronger six-state path construction succeeds everywhere.
This motivated the path-state lemma.

It has **not** yielded a general induction proving that every
cyclically-4 cubic macro has a path-state labelling.  As a diagnostic, the
same six-state search on every 3-edge-connected cubic macro through order
14 has one subrelation failure, on a graph with a nontrivial cyclic
3-edge cut; adding one compatible star state repairs it.  That UNSAT
diagnostic has no target significance and is not used in the theorem
above.

Since the cyclically-4 search produced no failure, there was no candidate
to expand through the direct target CNF/LRAT counterexample gate.

## Authorship, review, novelty, and publication

OpenAI Codex agents authored the programs, witness tables, and this note
under the user's direction.  “Independent” means separately implemented
checker code, not independent human authorship.  No human peer review has
yet occurred.

The six-state composition lemma and finite census may be useful research
infrastructure, but their novelty relative to multipole, Petersen
colouring, flow, and cycle-double-cover literature has not been
established.  This package is not a resolution and should not be submitted
or advertised as one without a literature review and independent human
verification.
