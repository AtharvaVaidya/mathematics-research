# Provisional novelty assessment

Date: 2026-07-29

Status: **plausibly new scoped counterexample; priority not established;
human literature review required**.

## Exact claim assessed

The manuscript constructs a simple connected bridgeless cubic graph on
162 vertices whose unique cardinality-minimum extendable binary
projection has size 54 and no clean extension. The graph nevertheless
has a five-cycle double cover.

This refutes a minimum-projection selection principle proposed inside
this project. It does not refute FiveCDC.

## Primary-source comparison

- Hušek and Šámal, *Exponentially Many Circuit Double Covers*,
  arXiv:2607.24724, give the immediate \(\mathbb F_2^3\)-flow
  component-parity criterion. Their paper does not state the strict-lock
  construction or the 162-vertex counterexample.
- Hoffmann-Ostenhof, *A Note on 5-Cycle Double Covers*, Graphs and
  Combinatorics 29 (2013), gives a necessary and sufficient condition
  for a prescribed 2-regular subgraph to occur in a five-cycle double
  cover.
- OpenAI's July 2026 proof, and expositions by Oum and Geelen, concern
  the ordinary Cycle Double Cover Theorem. The stronger five-member
  assertion remains separate. The present graph has a FiveCDC.
- Jin, Mazzuoccolo, and Steffen, *Cores, Joins and the Fano-Flow
  Conjectures*, discuss different Fano-flow objectives through cores,
  joins, and weak oddness. Their results are relevant context but do not
  appear to contain the minimum extendable-projection statement here.

## Caution

The targeted screen was performed immediately after the July 2026
Hušek--Šámal preprint appeared. It cannot exclude unpublished work,
parallel discoveries, or an equivalent result in different language.
The full-flow master lemma is elementary and receives no priority claim.
The strict-lock construction and minimized witness should be submitted
to specialists for comparison before any novelty language stronger than
"apparently new to this project."
