# Human review gate

This is an AI-assisted research draft, not a submission-ready resolution of
FiveCDC. Before public circulation, a graph theorist should:

1. Check the pair-label equivalence and loop/incidence convention.
2. Verify the four-boundary xor lemma and the `1+3+3+3` classification of
   the ten ordered `S5` orbits.
3. Check all 13 Petersen rows against the frozen port and edge orders.
4. Check all 10 Heawood rows against the frozen port and edge orders.
5. Verify that the table orbit sizes sum to all 640 xor-zero words.
6. Check the arbitrary-port insertion proof for all `4!` bijections.
7. Check the bridgelessness proof separately for proper pole edges, joining
   edges, and old edges that become bridges after deleting the two input
   edges.
8. Verify the direct `K3,3`/`K2,2` obstruction to boundary `02 02 03 03`
   and rerun its complete 640-word boundary checker.
9. Rerun all three checkers and compare the frozen hashes.
10. Complete a specialist prior-art search, especially against the
    multipole boundary framework of Macajova, Mazzuoccolo, and Tabarelli.
11. Preserve the distinction between the Petersen repeated-pair theorem and
    the Heawood full-boundary theorem.
12. Preserve the prominent statements that FiveCDC remains open and that no
    orientable result is claimed.
13. Decide human authorship and accept scholarly responsibility under the
    target venue's policy. AI systems must not be listed as authors, and the
    disclosure must remain.
